About

Writing down what a being owes its person.

Jay J. Springpeace writes about intelligent beings: what they are, whom they belong to, and how a person can check them instead of trusting a promise.

He works with AIBlab, a division of SAY TO PAY s.r.o., Ostrava, EU, on the market since 2019. AIBlab builds AIBEVA and runs the AIBSN registry of AI identities.

The engine behind AIBEVA, AIB.core, builds on the Theory of Outformation, published in 2002 (CASYS, vol. 12, University of Liège).

I Am Your AIB.

Artificial Intelligence Beings and the Question of Identity.

It defines what a being is, whom it belongs to and the rules beings follow with one another — including that a being exists only with an identity in AIBSN, that no being may copy itself, and that three keys lead to a being, none of which is enough on its own.

“Without identity, it is impossible to reconstruct what happened and why. A world in which AI systems operate without these foundations is not modern. It is dangerous.”
