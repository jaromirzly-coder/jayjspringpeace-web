About

BUILDING THE IDENTITY LAYER FOR THE AI ERA.

Jay J. Springpeace’s journey began with Computer Engineering studies and continued at Harvard University.

He later lectured at CASYS at the University of Liège, Belgium — one of the most distinctive AI research environments of its time. While mainstream AI followed the statistical path, the CASYS community asked different questions: Can a system carry responsibility across time? Can it have identity?

Those questions led to the Theory of Outformation (CASYS — International Journal of Computing Anticipatory Systems, vol. 12, University of Liège, 2002) and, years later, to the AIBSN registry and AIB.core — the engine behind AIBEVA.
